SimReader units

SimReader converts every report into these units, whatever the report itself uses (joules, GJ, MMBtu and so on), so baseline and proposed results always line up.

ElectricitykWhkW

Totals across energy types (for example “Baseline in kBtu” in Total Energy Performance) are in kBtu.

A gallon converts by its fuel: 137.381 kBtu for fuel oil and diesel, 91.452 kBtu for propane and 120.214 kBtu for gasoline. Other fuels in gallons use the fuel oil value.
