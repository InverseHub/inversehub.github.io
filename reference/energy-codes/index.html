Energy codes and compliance paths

Every energy code you can choose under Energy Code Used, with the compliance paths it offers in the Rating System list.

On this page
  • ASHRAE Standard 90.1
  • International Energy Conservation Code (IECC)
  • International Green Construction Code (IgCC)
  • State and city energy codes
  • Notes

Certifications and programs (LEED, NGBS, Enterprise Green Communities, Living Building Challenge, ILFI Zero Carbon, ILFI Zero Energy, Net Zero Energy Certifications, C-PACE and 179D) can be chosen with any code. A code marked superseded has a newer edition in effect, and stays available for projects permitted under it.

Notes

  • IECC and ASHRAE 90.1. An IECC edition allows the ASHRAE 90.1 edition of its time instead of its own requirements (IECC 2009: 90.1-2007; 2012: 90.1-2010; 2015: 90.1-2013; 2018: 90.1-2016; 2021: 90.1-2019). To use that alternative, choose the 90.1 edition as the Energy Code Used.
  • ASHRAE 90.1-2025 has no building performance factors in InverseHub yet.
  • IECC 2006 has no standard reference design tables in InverseHub yet, so Fill Baseline leaves envelope, fenestration and lighting for you.
  • State and city codes that change some baseline rules show a reminder in Fill Baseline to check the values against the code’s text.
