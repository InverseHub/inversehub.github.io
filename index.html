InverseHub turns your eQUEST and EnergyPlus results into one online report your team and clients can review: energy and cost by end use, energy code compliance and comments, phase by phase.

How it works

  1. Model as usual in eQUEST, EnergyPlus, OpenStudio or DesignBuilder, with utility rates in the model.
  2. Read and upload your baseline and proposed results with SimReader, a free Windows app.
  3. Finish the report in InverseHub: fill in the energy model inputs, then add the performance calculations and the conclusion.
  4. Share it: send the link, which anyone can comment on after confirming their email, invite reviewers by email, or let your client follow the project.
