Fill in Energy Model Inputs

Tell InverseHub which energy code and compliance path the project follows. It can then fill the baseline column of your model inputs from that code.

Energy Model Information

Select Edit on the card, change what you need, then Save.

FieldWhat to enter
Energy Model Based OnThe design stage the model reflects.
Simulation Program, Weather FileFilled in by the SimReader upload.
Principal Heating SourceThe proposed design’s main heating fuel. Fill Baseline uses it to pre-select HVAC systems.
Energy Code UsedType to search. Codes for your project’s state are listed first. The (i) next to the code summarizes what InverseHub does for it.
Climate ZoneUsed for the baseline tables and the building performance factors.
Rating SystemOnly the compliance paths of the code you chose are offered, plus certifications and programs such as LEED, NGBS or 179D.

The compliance paths by code:

  • ASHRAE 90.1, and the state and city codes built on it: Performance Rating Method (Appendix G) and Energy Cost Budget Method.
  • IECC: Total Building Performance. To use the ASHRAE 90.1 alternative an IECC edition allows, pick that 90.1 edition as the energy code instead.
  • IgCC: its Performance Option.
  • Codes that combine both, such as Chicago 2022 and New York State: all of their paths.

The full list is in Energy codes and compliance paths.

Energy Model Inputs

Each row has a Baseline and a Proposed column, by section: building envelope, fenestration, lighting, process loads, HVAC and domestic hot water. Use + on a section to add a row, and Edit to change one.

Fill the baseline from the energy code

  1. In Energy Model Inputs, open the menu (⋮) and select Fill Baseline from Energy Code. The name follows the path: Fill Budget Building… for the Energy Cost Budget Method, Fill Standard Reference Design… for Total Building Performance.

  2. The note at the top names the tables used. On each tab choose Add, Override or Don’t Apply, and tick the items to fill.
  3. Select Apply, then check the filled-in values.

Values with an x (for example “x.xx EER” or “U-0.XX”) are placeholders. Replace them with your project’s values. When a code changes some baseline rules, the dialog asks you to check the values against the code’s text.

Fill Baseline writes text into the Baseline column only. It doesn’t change your energy model or your uploaded results.
